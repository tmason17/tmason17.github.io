A Little About Us

Timeless Monkey / Charlotte, NC

Timeless Monkey started with a simple idea: finding a watch you like should not feel complicated. We wanted a small shop where people could compare a few styles, try on different straps, and leave with something that feels right.

The name is a reminder not to take style too seriously. Time matters, but so do the moments in between. We believe a watch can be useful, personal, and fun without needing to be expensive or showy.

Small Shop, Careful Choices

We focus on everyday watches that are comfortable, dependable, and easy to personalize.
