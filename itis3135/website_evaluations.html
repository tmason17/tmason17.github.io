Website Evaluations

Apple

Apple is an example of effective website design because it presents its products with clear organization and a consistent visual style.

  • Contrast: Apple uses contrasting text, backgrounds, and large product images to make important information stand out.
  • Repetition: The website consistently repeats its typography, navigation, buttons, and product presentation styles.
  • Alignment: Product images, headings, and buttons are positioned neatly, creating a clean and organized appearance.
  • Proximity: Product descriptions and their related buttons are grouped together, making it easy to understand which actions belong to each product.

Blinkee

Blinkee is an example of ineffective website design because its colorful and crowded layout makes it difficult to focus on important information and navigate products easily.

  • Contrast: Blinkee uses many bright colors, images, and text elements that compete for attention, making it difficult to identify the most important information.
  • Repetition: The website repeats product images and shopping categories, but the large amount of visual information makes the overall design feel cluttered.
  • Alignment: Product images, descriptions, and shopping information create a busy appearance. More consistent spacing and alignment would improve the organization of the page.
  • Proximity: Multiple products and categories appear close together, making it harder to distinguish individual items and locate related information.
